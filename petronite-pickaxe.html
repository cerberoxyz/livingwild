The Petronite Pickaxe is the mod's ultimate mining tool: tougher than netherite and with a haste ability of its own. A ring of crystals spins endlessly around its head.

Recipe

Petronite Ingot
Petronite Ingot
Petronite Ingot
Blaze Rod
Blaze Rod
→
Petronite Pickaxe

Ability: haste

Right-click → mining at 2× speed (like Haste V) for 5 seconds. 15-second cooldown.

Notes

  • It satisfies Minecraft's pickaxe tag.
  • It is the ideal tool for mining the Block of Petronite and Petronite Ore.

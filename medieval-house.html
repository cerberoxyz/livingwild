Medieval House

Medieval House
Data
DimensionOverworld
BiomesPlains, savanna, forest, birch forest, flower forest
Size47 × 33 × 40
HeightOn the surface (level with the ground)
Frequency1 every ~64 chunks (separation 28)
ContainsTraveling Merchant, Blacksmith and Fairy
IDlivingwild:casa_medieval

The Medieval House is the most common structure in the mod: a small medieval estate with lawns, paths and houses. It is the first target of the Castle Compass and the best place to start trading.

Inhabitants

  • Traveling Merchant: emeralds, gold and Ender pearls.
  • Blacksmith: iron swords, chestplates and shears.
  • Fairy: sells nothing, but is the guardian of the Fairy Heart.

Tips

  • NPCs that already live in a structure do not count toward the per-chunk spawn limit.
  • If you want to hire the blacksmith, bring 32 emeralds. See Hiring Companions.

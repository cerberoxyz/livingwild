Summary: The four elements —Fire, Ice, Earth and Wind— each have an adult dragon and a baby version. Dragons are hatched from the egg, grow with fish, breed with golden apples (and can produce hybrid variants), can be ridden and piloted in flight.

Life cycle

  1. Egg → found at the Dragon Portal (outer End).
  2. Hatching → next to a heat source, 5 minutes. See Dragon Egg.
  3. Baby → it only moves around and lies down to rest from time to time. Fed with raw fish.
  4. Breeding → two adults with a golden apple lay an egg (details).
  5. Adult → after 5 fish. It can be ridden and piloted. See Riding a Dragon.

Hybrid variants

When two adult dragons are bred, a variant with a mixed look may be born. It uses the powers of the indicated element.

Care

  • Heal: raw fish (+10 health, Regeneration II) or cooked fish (+25, Regeneration III) with a right-click on an adult.
  • Breed: golden apple to two nearby adults. See Dragon Breeding.
  • Escort: the awake adult defends its owner within a 30-block radius.
  • Turbo: Ctrl + W while flying, up to 5 s. See Riding a Dragon.

Owner

The owner is whoever placed the egg (the baby inherits it on hatching, and the adult inherits it from the baby). If an ownerless adult dragon is ridden with an empty hand, the first person to ride it becomes its owner.

Self-defense

An unridden adult dragon that is attacked by a player who is not its owner or by any mob (even with arrows) gets up, chases the aggressor and brings them down with its breath. Lethal on purpose: 100 damage. See Riding a Dragon › Self-defense.

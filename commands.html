Guides

Commands

LivingWild adds no commands of its own, but it works with Minecraft's and includes a test function.

Summon

CommandEffect
/summon livingwild:dragosoSummons Dragoso
/summon livingwild:boss_glacialSummons the Glacial Boss
/summon livingwild:dragon_fuego (_hielo, _tierra, _viento)Adult dragon
/summon livingwild:bebe_dragon_fuego (etc.)Baby dragon
/summon livingwild:dragon_fuego ~ ~ ~ {DragonVariant:1}Hybrid variant. DragonVariant: 1 Magma (uses dragon_fuego), 2 Blizzard (dragon_hielo), 3 Solar (dragon_viento), 4 Crystal (dragon_tierra). See Breeding
/summon livingwild:perro_esqueleto_dragon · ogro_playstation · tucan_humanoide · orangutan_pez · cerdo_fornidoHostile Creatures
/summon livingwild:caballero (and the rest of the NPC IDs)NPCs

Structures

  • /locate structure livingwild:casa_medieval (also castillo_en_la_luna, arena_glacial, portal_dragon).
  • /place template livingwild:casa_medieval ~ ~ ~ places the template where you stand.
  • /place structure livingwild:portal_dragon generates it in full.
  • /function livingwild:verificar_estructuras (operator): automatic test of all structures far from spawn (X=30000), unloading them when finished.

Items

/give @s livingwild:lingote_de_petronita, livingwild:espada_del_alba, livingwild:corazon_de_hada, etc. (the IDs appear in each item's infobox).

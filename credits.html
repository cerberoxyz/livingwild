Mod

LivingWild — a mod for Minecraft Java 26.3 (Fabric). License: see the project's LICENSE file.

Dependencies

  • GeckoLib (Tslat and contributors), MIT license.
  • Fabric API.

Third-party textures and models

  • Dawnblade: “Sunfire” texture from Simply Swords (Sweenus / Timefall Development), used with permission.
  • Stormaxe: “Runic Greataxe” texture from the Simply Swords resource pack (fsd), used with permission.
  • 3D armor: based on the “IAF_spellbooks-armorchangde” pack by Ai_myh (ask the author's permission before publishing).
  • Glacial Arena: medieval arena adapted from a community schematic (the original credit is on a sign in the structure).

Dragon Breeding

Contents
  1. How to breed them
  2. Probabilities
  3. Hybrid variants
  4. The egg
New in v31: two adult dragons can lay an egg of their own, and from it four hybrid variants with a unique look can hatch.

How to breed them

  1. You need two adult dragons, with no rider, awake and on the ground, less than 24 blocks from each other.
  2. Right-click with a golden apple (or enchanted) on each one. They fill with hearts and fall in love for 30 s; you must give both one within that time.
  3. They seek each other out. When they meet, one of them lays an egg nearby and the game notifies you.
  4. Both go into a 5-minute rest before they can breed again (the notice says how many seconds remain).
  5. The egg's owner is the owner of the dragon that laid it (or its mate's, if it has none).

Probabilities

ParentsResult
Fire + Earth50% Magma, 50% regular dragon (from one of the two parents)
Ice + Wind50% Blizzard, 50% regular dragon (from one of the two parents)
Fire + Wind50% Solar, 50% regular dragon (from one of the two parents)
Ice + Earth50% Crystal, 50% regular dragon (from one of the two parents)
Any other regular pair (same element, Fire + Ice, Earth + Wind)5% chance of a random variant, 95% regular dragon
Any hybrid parent40% inherits its variant (70% if both are the same); otherwise, a regular dragon

If the offspring comes out regular, it is the element of one of the two parents in equal parts.

Hybrid variants

Magma Dragon
Fire + Earth · Fire powers		Blizzard Dragon
Ice + Wind · Ice powers
Solar Dragon
Fire + Wind · Wind powers		Crystal Dragon
Ice + Earth · Earth powers

Each variant mixes two elements in its look, but uses the powers of only one (breath, ball and egg). They have the same stats, animations and controls as the regular dragons.

The egg

The egg laid by the dragons is the Dragon Egg of the offspring's element and looks the same as the regular one, even if it is a variant. It needs heat (within 3 blocks) and takes 5 minutes. When it hatches, a message like “A baby Magma dragon has hatched! (Fire and Earth hybrid)” appears.

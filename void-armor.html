The Void Armor is the evolution of the Petronite Armor: a darker version with horns, glowing eyes, a plated mask, a rune on the forehead, a 3D muzzle with ivory fangs and side mantles. All of its values are double those of Petronite.

Full set bonus

+8 hearts (16 health in total) and +40% speed.

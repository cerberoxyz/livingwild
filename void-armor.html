Void Armor

Void Armor
3D Model
Information
TypeArmor (4 pieces)
RarityEpic
ObtainedSmithing upgrade
IDmundovivo:vacio_casco · _pechera · _pantalones · _botas
Stats
Defense8 / 16 / 12 / 8 (44)
Durability814 / 1184 / 1110 / 962
Toughness8.0 per piece
Knockback res.0.2 per piece
Enchantability18
Repaired withPetronite or netherite ingot

The dark evolution of Petronite Armor: purple Petronite model with Void horns, glowing eyes, plated mask and a 3D snout. Every stat is exactly double Petronite (derived in code).

Bonuses

  • Per piece: +4 max health (2 hearts) and +2 armor.
  • Full set: +16 max health in total (+8 hearts) and +40 % speed.
  • Full set: no fall damage up to 16 blocks.

Full-set abilities (from Dragon Lord)

StepActionDetails
1Jump in midairDouble jump: vertical boost 0.85 plus particles and a sound
2Land after a double jump (drop ≥ 4 blocks)Shockwave: radius 7.5, 20 damage, launches enemies up (0.75); flames, smoke, lava particles
3Jump again in midairFlight starts (elytra-style glide, no elytra needed)
4Jump while flyingBoost forward, 4 s cooldown; retractable wings flap

Disabled in water, riding, with levitation or while holding creative flight (e.g. Dragon Wings hovering).

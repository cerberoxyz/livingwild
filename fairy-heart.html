A totem-style heart with wings and a rune. Works from main or off hand.

Behavior

  • 3 charges. Each rescue uses one; after the third it breaks.
  • Rescue: health set to 10 (5 hearts), all effects cleared, Regeneration II for 45 s, Absorption II for 5 s, fire extinguished.
  • The animation is the vanilla Totem of Undying activation (with totem sound and particles).
  • Holding it also gives Regeneration I and Speed I (refreshed every 2 s).
  • The Fairy no longer sells it: it was a trade before v12.15 and is a crafting recipe now.

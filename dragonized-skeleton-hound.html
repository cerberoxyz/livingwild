Dragonized Skeleton Hound

Dragonized Skeleton Hound
3D Model
Information
TypeHostile (Monster)
SpawnsOverworld (any biome), weight 25, groups 1–2
IDmundovivo:perro_esqueleto_dragon
Attributes
Health28
Attack damage5
Armor2
Speed0.34
Knockback res.0.0
Follow range10
Hitbox0.9 × 1.3
Fire immuneYes

One of the five original hostile mobs. They attack players they see within 10 blocks with line of sight, retaliate when hit, and need darkness, their biome and a difficulty above Peaceful to spawn naturally. Sounds: Skeleton sounds.

Drops

ItemChance / amount
Bones2–4 (looting)
Dragon's Breath35 %
Petronite Ore12 %, 1–2
Dragon Head2 %

Animations

Idle, walk, run (when speed exceeds the walking reference), attack (on hit), hurt and death (kept for 32 ticks).

/summon mundovivo:perro_esqueleto_dragon ~ ~ ~

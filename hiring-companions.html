Guides

Hiring Companions

Important: Almost all NPCs can join you as combat companions. The Fairy cannot: she flies and “cannot accompany you”.

How to hire

  1. Right-click the NPC.
  2. Press “Hire: 32 emeralds” (the cost is configurable, see Configuration).
  3. The items are taken from your inventory and the NPC becomes your companion.

What a companion does

  • Follows you and teleports near you if you get more than 24 blocks away.
  • Defends you: attacks whoever hurts you and the enemies you attack.
  • Carries a weapon in the main hand:
BuildNPCsWeapon
StrongKnight, Blacksmith, City Guard, Archer, FarmerStormaxe
SlimWizard, Traveling Merchant, Alchemist, Player, Monk, BardDawnblade
  • Their base damage drops to 1 while they are a companion, because the weapon already adds a lot.

Companion controls

ButtonEffect
Follow me / Stay hereToggles between following you and waiting in place.
DismissReleases them and takes the weapon away.

Only the owner can give orders; if another player talks to them they will say “I already work for someone else.”. The lines are in NPC Quotes.

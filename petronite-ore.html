Petronite Ore

Petronite Ore
Information
TypeBlock (ore)
Obtained fromNatural generation
IDlivingwild:mena_de_petronita
Stats
Hardness6,0
Blast resistance600
Light level7
ToolDiamond pickaxe or better

The Petronite Ore is the mineral that gives rise to Petronite. It emits a faint blue glow (light level 7), which helps you spot it in the darkness of caves.

Generation

DetailValue
HeightY −30 to Y −20
ReplacesStone and deepslate
Vein sizeup to 4 blocks
Attempts per chunk4
DimensionWorld surface (Overworld)

Mining

It needs a diamond pickaxe (or better) to drop its loot. It drops Raw Petronite.

Technical note: According to the mod's current data, the block's loot table does not apply Fortune or Silk Touch.

Tips

  • Dig in deep caves at the height of diamond veins, a little above.
  • The blue glow is easier to see in low light: put out the torches to scan walls.
  • If you can't find anything, check the FAQ: the veins are small.

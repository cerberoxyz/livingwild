Dragoso is LivingWild's main boss: a half-dragon, half-bear beast with horns, antlers, a mane, a halo of segments above its head and enormous arms. It is summoned with a ritual similar to the Wither's, but with different blocks. When it appears, a message announces: “DRAGOSO HAS AWAKENED!”.

Summoning

A row of 3 netherrack, 1 Block of Petronite under the middle one and 3 End dragon heads on top. The two spaces beside the petronite block must be empty. See Summoning the Bosses.

When you place the last head the pattern disappears and the boss rises from the ground (spawn animation, ~3.6 s invulnerable). Also with its spawn egg or with /summon livingwild:dragoso.

Phases

Phase 1 (above 50% health)

It walks and uses its melee attacks and ranged fireballs.

Phase 2 — enraged (below 50%)

It lets out a furious roar, runs faster, chains longer combos, shortens the waits between attacks and takes off to chase you through the air.

Attacks

Roar814-block wave that pushes you back; now with damage.
Tail whip—Circular attack around itself.
Leap22It jumps and lands on you with an 8.5-block shockwave.
Charge18A run at ~18 blocks/s that follows you; if it hits you, it chains a bite.
Fireballs—One for every player within 70 blocks, 3 waves per attack, they explode without breaking blocks.
Flight and dive—It takes off if you are far away (26+ blocks), if it has no path or if you are very high; it lands on top of you with a shockwave.

Combos

It chains attacks: bite → swipe, swipe → bite → ground slam, etc. (longer when enraged). If you escape its range, the combo is cancelled.

Against groups

If 3 or more players are within 10 blocks: it performs a ground slam to push them away, retreats for ~5 s and fires fireballs at everyone.

Rewards

  • 50 experience levels for all players within 96 blocks of its death (and for whoever finishes it, even from afar).
  • Dragon Wings (always). Its loot table is empty: the wings are dropped by code.

Strategies

  • Fight in the open with a full set of Void Armor and the Fairy Heart in your off hand.
  • The Arcane Staff does not hurt players: use it to damage him from a distance with the blast.
  • Don't stand still during the fireballs: every player gets their own.
  • In phase 2 avoid being more than 26 blocks away; otherwise it will take off and dive at you.
  • With companions (Hiring Companions) you can split its attention.

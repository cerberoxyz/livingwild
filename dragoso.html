Dragoso

The first great boss: a colossal dragon summoned with a Wither-style altar. Spawn animation lasts about 3.6 s of invulnerability.

Phases

  • Phase 1 (health above 50 %): walks, closes distance, uses claw swipe, bite, slam, tail whip, roar and fireballs.
  • Phase 2 / enraged (below 50 %): roars, runs faster, shorter cooldowns, longer combos, may take off, circle-fly and dive onto you.

Attacks

AttackDetails
Swipe / Slam / Tail whipClose-range hits that affect everyone nearby; lunges toward you while winding up
Bite21 damage, lunges forward (attack_bite); combos: bite → swipe, swipe → bite → slam
Charge18 damage at about 18 blocks/s, follows you; chains a bite on hit. Cooldown 8.5 s (5.5 s enraged)
Leap22 damage with shockwave when it lands. Cooldown 7 s (4.5 s enraged)
RoarNow deals damage
FireballsCooldown 8 s (5 s enraged); one per player within 70 blocks, 3 waves, explode without breaking blocks
Flight chaseTakes off when you are 26+ blocks away for ~2 s, stuck, or very high; lands with a shockwave
Crowd control3+ players within 10 blocks: ground slam to push them away, retreats ~5 s and fires fireballs

It re-evaluates its target every 0.5 s and switches to the nearest player. Step height is 0.6 so it cannot climb tall blocks.

Rewards

Every player within 96 blocks gets 50 levels (and whoever lands the kill). Dragon Wings always drop (by code; the loot table is empty).

Summoning

See Summoning the Bosses. Also /summon mundovivo:dragoso.

The Magma Dragon is a hybrid of Fire and Earth: Earth body and tail, fire head and wings. Only appears by breeding (variant ID 1).

Powers

It uses the Fire powers (breath, fireball, egg and entity type); only the look differs.

Summon: /summon mundovivo:dragon_fuego ~ ~ ~ {DragonVariant:1}

Life cycle

  • Egg → needs heat, hatches in 5 min → baby.
  • Baby eats 5 raw fish (1 min apart) → adult.
  • Adult can be ridden and bred.

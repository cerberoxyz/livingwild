Structures

Structures

Summary: LivingWild adds four structures. Since they are hard to spot by eye, the Castle Compass exists.
StructureDimensionBiomesSize (X×Y×Z)Frequency (chunks)Contains
Medieval HouseOverworldPlains, savanna, forest, birch forest, flower forest47 × 33 × 40every ~64 (separation 28)Traveling Merchant, Blacksmith, Fairy
Castle on the MoonOverworld (floating, Y 160)Any Overworld biome46 × 86 × 61every ~640 (separation 400)Wizard, Knight, Fairy
Glacial ArenaOverworldSnowy biomes117 × 33 × 123every ~36 (separation 16)Glacial Boss
Dragon PortalEnd (outer islands)End highlands, midlands, small islands and barrens56 × 59 × 62every ~40 (separation 16)Dragon Egg

Frequency uses Minecraft's random_spread system: one structure per “spacing” chunks cell, separated by at least “separation” chunks.

Locating structures

  • Castle Compass: 2560-block range; change target by sneaking.
  • Vanilla command: /locate structure livingwild:casa_medieval (also castillo_en_la_luna, arena_glacial, portal_dragon). See Commands.
Heads up: Structures only generate in new chunks. If you install the mod in an already explored world, you will have to travel to ungenerated areas.

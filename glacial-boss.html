Glacial Boss

Glacial Boss
3D Model
Information
TypeBoss (Monster)
SpawnGlacial Arena, altar or egg
Boss barBlue
IDmundovivo:boss_glacial
Attributes
Health4000
Attack damage44 (×3.0 scripted)
Armor22
Knockback res.1.0
Speed0.36 (chase ×1.6 / ×1.9 enraged)
Follow range128
Hitbox3.6 × 4.6 (model scale 0.5)
Rewards
XP100 levels within 100 blocks
DropsDragon Wings + Petronite Block (always)

A frost beast with 2400 health in v12.8 that was reinforced to 4000 in v12.9.

Attacks

AttackDetails
BiteReach 5 blocks
Ground slamReach 6.5 blocks
Tail whipReach 6.5 blocks
Frost roarReach 9 blocks
Ice rainIce falls on every player within 32 blocks; a second strike below 50 % health
  • Melee attacks only below 5.5 blocks. Re-targets every 5 ticks and only switches if another player is 8 blocks closer.
  • If no path exists it advances in a straight line.
  • Pause between attacks 8–13 ticks (3–6 enraged).

Fighting it

It guards the center of the Glacial Arena, or you can summon it yourself (altar).

The Glacial Boss is a colossal beast of ice and white fur with horns, a crest of blue spikes and a body made of thousands of cubes. It rises from a base of cracked ice. It appears at the center of the Glacial Arena or can be summoned manually.

Summoning

Right-click the petronite block while holding a Nether Star (it is consumed). See Summoning the Bosses.

Attacks

AttackDamageDescription
Bite245-block, 45° cone.
Ground slam266.5-block shockwave.
Tail whip206.5-block, 100° cone with heavy knockback.
Frost roar89-block wave that pushes you back.
Ice rainvariableFalls on every player within 32 blocks, aiming ahead of your movement. Below 50% health, a second strike lands on the same spots.
  • Aggressive chase: it re-evaluates its target every half second and does not switch players unless another is clearly closer.
  • Below 50% health: it roars, speeds up and cuts the wait between attacks to 3–6 ticks (8–13 normally).

Rewards

  • 100 experience levels for every player within 100 blocks.
  • Dragon Wings and a Block of Petronite, always.

Strategies

  • Use Void Armor and bring food, the Fairy Heart and a shield: some attacks cover a large area.
  • The ice rain predicts your movement: change direction sharply at the last moment.
  • Stay more than 5 blocks away to dodge the bite; more than 6.5 for the ground slam.

The Dragon Wings are membranous dragon wings worn in the chest slot (they give no defense) and allow flight. They use an animated 3D model: they open when you glide and stay open.

Note: The internal ID is alas_de_hada for historical reasons (the Fairy used to sell them), but the displayed name is “Dragon Wings”.

How to get them

The wings cannot be crafted: they always drop when you defeat Dragoso and the Glacial Boss.

Usage

  • Jump and press jump again to glide, like with an elytra.
  • Right-click with a bone while gliding: speed boost.
  • While worn: +10% speed and Jump Boost I.
  • Repair them with phantom membrane at an anvil.

Compatibility

The wings follow the player's pose when animated by mods such as EMF, Fresh Animations or Punchy Moves. See Compatibility.

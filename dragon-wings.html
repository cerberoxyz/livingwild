Dragon Wings

Dragon Wings
3D Model
Information
TypeWings (chest slot)
RarityEpic
ObtainedDragoso and Glacial Boss drops
IDmundovivo:alas_de_hada
Stats
Durability3200
Defense0
Repaired withPhantom Membrane
Ability
EffectCreative-style levitation

Membranous wings worn in the chest slot. Internal ID alas_de_hada (historical).

Levitation (not elytra)

  • Since the port from 1.20.1 they are not an elytra: no gliding.
  • Double-tap jump to lift off like creative flight. Jump rises, sneak descends, release to hover in place.
  • Flight speed is very low (0.015).
  • Costs 1 Bone every 5 seconds in the air; a bone is needed to start. No bones, no levitation.
  • While worn: +10 % speed and Jump Boost I.
  • Camera pitches slightly while hovering; sprint does not speed you up.

Obtaining

Not craftable. Dropped every time by the Dragoso and the Glacial Boss. Also a valid base for the Void Chestplate upgrade.

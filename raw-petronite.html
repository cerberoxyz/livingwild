Raw Petronite

Raw Petronite
Information
TypeMaterial
Obtained fromMine Petronite Ore
IDlivingwild:petronita_bruta

The Raw Petronite (internal ID petronita_bruta) is the unprocessed mineral. It is smelted to turn it into Refined Petronite Ore — the same item with a different look —, which is the real ingredient in the recipes.

Refining

IngredientBlockResultTimeXP
Raw PetroniteFurnace / Blast furnaceRefined Petronite Ore10 s2,0

It works in both the furnace and the blast furnace.

Uses

  • Refined ore + diamond → Petronite Ingot.
  • 8 refined ores in a ring → Arcane Staff.

Loot

The Dragonized Skeleton Hound also drops it with a low chance (12%, 1–2 units).

Important: Unrefined ore does not work in the recipes: it must go through the furnace first.

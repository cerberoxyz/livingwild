The Petronite Armor uses a 3D ice-and-steel model: a frost wolf skull with fangs and curved horns, spiked shoulder pads, a long cape and claws.

Full set bonus

+4 hearts (8 health in total) and +20% speed.

Upgrade

Each piece can be upgraded to the Void Armor at the smithing table.

Compatibility

The armor takes the animated pose from EMF, Fresh Animations, Punchy Moves and other animation mods. See Compatibility.

Migration notice: The old IDs netherite_reforzado_* were renamed to petronita_* in v12.11: items with the old ID disappear in worlds saved before that change.

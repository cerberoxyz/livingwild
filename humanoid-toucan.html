Humanoid Toucan

The Humanoid Toucan is one of the five hostile creatures of the mod. A toucan with an enormous beak that walks upright like a person. Faster than strong, it lives in groups.

Behavior

It attacks players it detects within 10 blocks and defends itself if hit. If you set it on fire, some of its drops come out cooked.

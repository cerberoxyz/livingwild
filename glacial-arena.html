Glacial Arena

Glacial Arena
Data
DimensionOverworld
BiomesSnowy plains, ice spikes, snowy taiga, snowy slopes, grove, snowy beach
Size117 × 33 × 123
HeightOn the surface
Frequency1 every ~36 chunks (separation 16)
ContainsGlacial Boss (center of the sand floor)
IDlivingwild:arena_glacial

The Glacial Arena is a large medieval coliseum in the snowy biomes. At the center of its sand floor waits the Glacial Boss, which appears with every generated arena.

Preparation

  • Bring Void Armor or the best you have, food and the Fairy Heart.
  • It is a wide, open space: ideal for dodging the ice rain.
  • It is not a Castle Compass target; use /locate structure livingwild:arena_glacial or explore the snowy biomes.
  • The boss can also be summoned by hand. See Summoning the Bosses.

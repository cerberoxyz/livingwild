The Crystal Dragon is a hybrid variant that is only born when you breed two adult dragons. Ice body and wings; Earth tail and head. It has its own model and texture and is ridden, flies and sleeps just like the regular dragons.

Powers

Only the looks change: the breath, the ball, the egg type and the stats are those of the Earth element. It applies Poison II: 3 s (6 s with the ball).

How to get it

  1. Get an adult (regular) Ice Dragon and an adult (regular) Earth Dragon.
  2. Give each one a golden apple, with less than 24 blocks between them.
  3. One of the two lays an egg: there is a 50% chance it is Crystal and a 50% chance a regular dragon comes out.
  4. Give the egg heat (fire, lava, magma or a lit campfire) and wait 5 minutes. When it hatches, a notice tells you what came out.
  5. Give the baby 5 raw fish so it grows. See Dragon Breeding.

Baby

The Baby Crystal Dragon has 40 health and behaves like any baby: it walks, lies down to rest, does not defend itself and cannot be ridden. When it grows up it keeps its variant. The egg looks like the Earth one.

Inheritance

If one of the parents is already a hybrid, the offspring has a 40% chance of inheriting its variant (70% if both parents are the same). If it does not inherit it, a regular dragon is born. See the full table in Dragon Breeding.

Test command

/summon livingwild:dragon_tierra ~ ~ ~ {DragonVariant:4} (and livingwild:bebe_dragon_tierra for the baby). Variants have no ID of their own: they are the Earth dragon with the DragonVariant tag.

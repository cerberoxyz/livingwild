Version History

Summarized from the mod’s change notes (CAMBIOS_*.md). Most versions were verified statically; always test in game.

VersionChanges
v41Compile fixes for the 26.3 port.
v39–v40Port of the 1.20.1 v13 features: dragons follow owner, Dragon Wings levitation, Void armor abilities, Fairy Heart 3 charges, Blood Tempest model.
v36–v38Stormaxe sideways display, new tool colors, dragon-head axe, first-person tuning.
v33Renamed Livingwild; English default plus Spanish.
v31Hybrid dragons, breeding, turbo cap, dragons stop hurting each other.
v30Fish healing, escort, stronger breath, second rider.
v29Head/body signs, barrel-roll tilt, sleeping pose.
v28Dragon self-defense.
v27Shift + right-click sleep; Ender chest removed.
v26No fire on mount; wings keep flapping.
v25Rider tilts with dragon.
v24Head follows rider’s look.
v23Barrel-roll-style camera.
v22Banking, landing.
v21Two riders, castle compass target 3.
v20Hatching, growth, riding, fire.
v19Dragons, eggs, Dragon Portal.
v12.14–12.15New Dawnblade throw; Fairy Heart recipe.
v12.11–12.13Void armor, Glacial rebalance, tools, ID rename.
v12.7–12.10Ice armor, castles rarer, Dragoso buff, Glacial Boss, arena.
v12.6Armor compat, rarer structures, mob spawn weights.
v12.2–12.4Fairy rework, mobs hostile, 3D eggs.
v12Five hostile mobs, loot, Dragoso locomotion.
v11.3–11.9Structure fixes (NBT lists), compass, Fairy Heart animation.

Version History

Note: Summary based on the mod's changelog files. Some versions were tested in-game and others only through static review.
VersionChanges
v11.3–v11.7Structure repairs (NBT templates), new Castle Compass.
v12Hostile mobs, loot and Dragoso locomotion.
v12.2The Fairy only offers the Fairy Heart; the 5 mobs attack on sight (10 blocks).
v12.43D spawn eggs.
v12.7Ice armor redesigned; castles much rarer.
v12.8Stronger Dragoso (3000 health) and new Glacial Boss.
v12.9–v12.10Glacial Arena.
v12.11Void Armor; Glacial rebalance; IDs netherite_reforzado_* → petronita_*.
v12.14New Dawnblade with throwing.
v17Dragoso: chase and melee.
v18Animated wings and pickaxe.
v19Dragons (adults and babies), 3D eggs and the Dragon Portal.
v20Hatching, growth, riding and fire for the dragons.
v212-player riding and compass.
v23“Do a Barrel Roll”-style flight.
v24–v25Head that points where the rider looks; rider tilted with the dragon.
v26No fire while riding; the wings keep flapping.
v27Sleeping the dragon (Shift + right-click); the Ender chest is removed.
v28The dragon defends itself (100 damage).
v29Head and body with correct signs, Barrel Roll-style tilt and lying-down sleep.
v30Healing with fish, slow fall when descending in the air, stronger breath (8 damage), dragon escort and second rider fixed. Turbo with Ctrl + W.
v31Hybrid variants (Magma, Blizzard, Solar, Crystal) and dragon breeding; turbo limited to 5 s with a cooldown and slower normal flight; dragons no longer damage each other.

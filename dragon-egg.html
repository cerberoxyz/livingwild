Dragon Egg

Dragon Egg

The Dragon Eggs are blocks with an animated 3D model that can be picked up, carried and placed. There is one per element, and each hatches a baby dragon of its element.

FireIceEarthWind
Fire DragonIce DragonEarth DragonWind Dragon

Where to get them

Only at the Dragon Portal, a structure that generates on the outer End islands. There are 4 variants (25% each), each with its egg at the center of the portal's lower edge.

Breeding eggs

Besides the Dragon Portal, two adult dragons can lay an egg of their own with a golden apple. It looks like the offspring's element egg, but may hide a hybrid variant (Magma, Blizzard, Solar or Crystal). See Dragon Breeding. When it hatches, a notice tells you what came out.

Hatching

  1. Place the egg near a heat source (within 3 blocks): fire, lava, a magma block or a lit campfire. In the Nether it is always warm.
  2. With heat, it takes 5 minutes (6000 ticks). Without heat it stops, but progress does not go back.
  3. In the last 10 s it shakes (wobble animation).
  4. It breaks the shell (hatch animation, 4 s) and the baby dragon of the same element comes out.
  5. The baby has the player who placed the egg as its owner.

Growth

The baby grows into an adult when fed raw fish (cod, salmon or tropical fish):

  • 5 fish are needed, with a 1-minute pause between each one (minimum ~4 minutes).
  • The adult inherits the owner.

See Dragons and Riding a Dragon.

Monk

Monk
Information
TypeNPC (Civilian)
SpawnsStructures and spawn egg
IDlivingwild:monje
Attributes
Health40
Base damage3
Speed0,3
Hitbox0.6 × 1.8

The Monk is one of the 12 NPCs of LivingWild. A serene monk who seeks peace in the simplest things.

Dialogue

  • “Inner peace begins with an apple.”
  • “Give me four apples and I'll give you a golden apple.”
  • “May the light be with you on your journey.”

Trades

You giveYou receive
4× Apple1× Golden Carrot
2× Honey Bottle4× Honeycomb
16× Melon Slice1× Golden Apple

As a companion

You can hire them for 32 emeralds (default value): they fight by your side and wield the Dawnblade.

All the dialogue of the 12 NPCs, exactly as it appears in the game (every time you talk to them or they say something above their head).

Knight

  • “For the honor of the crown.”
  • “I have sworn to protect this road.”
  • “Whoever forges their sword with petronite fears no darkness.”

Wizard

  • “The runes whisper if you know how to listen.”
  • “Beware of staring into the void for too long.”
  • “Petronite holds an ancient magic deep underground.”

Fairy

  • “What a lovely day for flying!”
  • “A Fairy Heart is forged from a totem, diamonds, gold and Ender pearls.”
  • “The forests tell me secrets when the fireflies glow.”

Traveling Merchant

  • “Whatever you're looking for, I have it... almost!”
  • “Emeralds, please, the road is long.”
  • “Gold, emeralds or a good deal, I'll take anything.”

Blacksmith

  • “Iron isn't tamed with words.”
  • “Give me ten ingots and I'll make you a worthy sword.”
  • “My hammer hasn't rested since dawn.”

Archer

  • “My aim never fails, except when I'm hungry.”
  • “Bring me string and I'll give you plenty of arrows.”
  • “The forest is my home, and you are welcome.”

Farmer

  • “This year's harvest was generous.”
  • “Are you hungry? Bring wheat and I'll give you bread.”
  • “Watch out for zombies near the river.”

Alchemist

  • “Every liquid has a secret, and I know them all.”
  • “Give me redstone dust and I'll give you bottles of experience.”
  • “Don't drink anything you haven't brewed yourself.”

Bard

  • “I have a ballad for every adventurer who passes by.”
  • “Want to hear a story? Bring a cookie and I'll tell it.”
  • “Great legends begin in a tavern like this one.”

City Guard

  • “Halt. Identify yourself, traveler.”
  • “This city is protected. Keep the peace.”
  • “They say there's a glowing blue ore beneath the deepslate.”

Player (NPC)

  • “Hi! Exploring too?”
  • “Let's go together, two swords are better than one.”
  • “Emeralds for meat, deal?”

Monk

  • “Inner peace begins with an apple.”
  • “Give me four apples and I'll give you a golden apple.”
  • “May the light be with you on your journey.”

Companion quotes

  • When hired: “Deal! From now on I fight by your side.” / “Let's go, adventurer!”
  • When following you: “I'm following you.” / “I'm following you, boss.”
  • When staying: “I'll stay here and wait for you.” / “I'll wait for you here.”
  • When dismissed: “Thanks for everything. I'll be on my way.”
  • If they already work for someone else: “I already work for someone else.”

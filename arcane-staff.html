Arcane Staff

The ore must be refined (smelted).

Right-click: poison beam

  • A purple particle beam reaches 12 blocks and pierces everything on its path.
  • Every living target (not players, not NPCs) gets Poison II and Weakness I for 10 s.
  • Cooldown 4 s.

Left-click: arcane blast

  • Sent by the client to the server; it replaces the normal attack and never breaks blocks.
  • Looking directly at a visible enemy (≤ 20 blocks): 16 damage to it alone.
  • Otherwise: 10 damage to every enemy within 20 blocks.
  • Knocks targets back (0.8 horizontal, 0.35 vertical). Cooldown 2 s.

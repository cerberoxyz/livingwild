Summoning the Bosses

Requirement: Both rituals need a Block of Petronite in the center. Have your best armor ready before you start.

Dragoso

Dragoso

Like the Wither, but with different blocks. Side view:

Dragon headDragon headDragon head
NetherrackNetherrackNetherrack
(empty)Block of Petronite(empty)
  • 3 netherrack in a row (along X or Z), 1 petronite block under the middle one and 3 End dragon heads on top.
  • The two spaces beside the petronite block must be empty.
  • Place the heads last: when you place the final one, the pattern disappears and the boss comes out (~3.6 s invulnerable).
  • If it fails, the message “Dragoso could not be summoned. The altar was not consumed.” appears on screen and you lose nothing.
  • Alternatives: spawn egg (creative) or /summon livingwild:dragoso.

Glacial Boss

View from above (everything at the same level):

Blue iceBlock of PetroniteBlue ice
Packed iceBlue icePacked ice
  • Right-click the petronite block with a Nether Star in hand (it is consumed).
  • Announcement: “THE GLACIAL BOSS HAS AWAKENED!”.
  • It also appears naturally in the Glacial Arena, with its spawn egg or with /summon livingwild:boss_glacial.

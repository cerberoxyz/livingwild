Alchemist

Alchemist
Information
TypeNPC (Civilian)
SpawnsStructures and spawn egg
IDlivingwild:alquimista
Attributes
Health40
Base damage3
Speed0,3
Hitbox0.6 × 1.8

The Alchemist is one of the 12 NPCs of LivingWild. An alchemist obsessed with potions.

Dialogue

  • “Every liquid has a secret, and I know them all.”
  • “Give me redstone dust and I'll give you bottles of experience.”
  • “Don't drink anything you haven't brewed yourself.”

Trades

You giveYou receive
16× Redstone Dust4× Bottle o' Enchanting
4× Spider Eye1× Fermented Spider Eye
8× Glowstone Dust8× Glass Bottle

As a companion

You can hire them for 32 emeralds (default value): they fight by your side and wield the Dawnblade.

Progression Guide

Contents

General idea: A recommended order. Nothing forces you to follow it, but each phase prepares the next.

Phase 1 — Petronite

  1. Mine Petronite Ore at Y −30…−20 with a diamond pickaxe.
  2. Smelt the Raw Petronite and combine it with a diamond: Petronite Ingot.
  3. Craft the Petronite Pickaxe (3 ingots + 2 blaze rods) to mine faster.

Phase 2 — Gear

  1. Dawnblade (2 ingots + blaze rod) and Stormaxe.
  2. Petronite Armor: 5 / 8 / 7 / 4 ingots (helmet / chestplate / leggings / boots).
  3. Arcane Staff with 8 refined ores.
  4. Fairy Heart: totem + 3 diamonds + 2 Ender pearls + 3 gold ingots.
  5. Castle Compass to find structures.

Phase 3 — First boss

  1. Gather netherrack and 3 End dragon heads (defeated dragon, or rare drops from dragonized hounds).
  2. Summon Dragoso (Summoning the Bosses).
  3. Get the Dragon Wings: now you can fly.

Phase 4 — Void Armor

Upgrade each piece at the smithing table with a netherite template and its material:

  • Helmet: echo shard · Chestplate: Nether Star · Leggings: shulker shell · Boots: dragon's breath.

See Void Armor.

Phase 5 — Glacial Boss

Face it in the Glacial Arena or summon it. With the full Void Armor you will have +40% speed, 16 extra health and lots of defense.

Phase 6 — Dragons

Travel to the outer End, find a Dragon Portal, hatch the egg, raise the baby with 5 fish and ride it.

Phase 7 — Companions

Hire NPCs (32 emeralds) to accompany you.

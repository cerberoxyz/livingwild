Catalog: All of the mod's items appear in the LivingWild creative tab. Here is the full catalog with how to obtain each one.

Petronite materials

ItemWhat it isHow to obtain it
Petronite OreOre blockUnderground, Y −30 to −20
Raw PetroniteRaw / refined oreMine the ore; the refined one is smelted in a furnace
Petronite IngotThe mod's main materialRefined ore + 1 diamond
Block of PetroniteStorage block and summoning hub9 ingots

Weapons and tools

ItemTypeAbility
DawnbladeSwordThrown like a boomerang (up to 50 blocks)
StormaxeAxeSummons lightning on the enemy
Arcane StaffMagic staffPoison beam and area blast
Petronite PickaxePickaxeMining at double speed for 5 s

Gear and utility

ItemTypeEffect
Fairy HeartTotemSaves you from death (5 charges) and gives Regeneration and Speed
Dragon WingsWings (chest)Elytra-style flight, +10% speed, Jump Boost
Petronite ArmorArmor (4 pieces)+health, +armor and +20% speed with the set
Void ArmorArmor (4 pieces)Double everything: +40% speed with the set
Castle CompassCompassLocates houses, castles and dragon portals

Blocks

  • Dragon Egg (Fire, Ice, Earth and Wind)
  • Petronite Ore and Block of Petronite

Spawn eggs

The 12 NPCs, Dragoso, the Glacial Boss and the 5 hostile creatures have a spawn egg in the creative tab. Dragons do not have a spawn egg: they are obtained by hatching their egg.

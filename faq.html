Guides

FAQ

Where do I find petronite?
Underground at Y −30…−20, in stone or deepslate. The veins are small (up to 4 blocks, 4 attempts per chunk): put out the torches to see their blue glow. See Petronite Ore.
Why doesn't the ore work in recipes?
It has to be refined first in a furnace or blast furnace. See Raw Petronite.
How do I get the Dragon Wings?
They can't be crafted: they drop when you defeat Dragoso or the Glacial Boss.
How do I get the Fairy Heart?
It is crafted (see Fairy Heart); the Fairy no longer sells it.
Can all the creatures be hired?
All the NPCs except the Fairy. See Hiring Companions.
Why does my dragon, or someone else's, kill me?
An unridden adult dragon defends itself against players who are not its owner with 100 damage. See Riding a Dragon › Self-defense.
How do I put my dragon to sleep?
Shift + right-click with an empty hand (owner only).
How do I get a hybrid dragon (Magma, Blizzard, Solar, Crystal)?
Give golden apples to two nearby adult dragons; with the right pair there is a 50% chance the egg is the variant. See Dragon Breeding.
How do I heal my dragon?
Right-click with raw fish (+10 health) or cooked fish (+25). See Healing and escort.
How do I fly faster?
Hold Ctrl + W: turbo for up to 5 s, with a 5 s cooldown. See Turbo.
My dragons were disappearing
It was a bug fixed in v31: an ownerless dragon could be brought down by another dragon. Now dragons do not damage each other.
Structures don't appear
They only generate in new chunks and in their biomes. Use the Castle Compass or /locate. See Structures.
The old armor items disappeared
The netherite_reforzado_* IDs were renamed to petronita_* in v12.11. See Petronite Armor.
Does it work with other mods?
See Compatibility.

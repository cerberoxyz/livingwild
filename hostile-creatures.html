Hostile Creatures

Hostile Creatures

Five original, eccentric creatures that appear at night and in caves, like any monster. All of them attack players on sight (10-block detection range) and defend themselves if you hit them.

Spawns

  • Monster category: they require darkness (night or caves), their biome and a difficulty other than Peaceful.
  • Vanilla weight reference: zombie 95, skeleton 100, creeper 100, spider 100, enderman 10. LivingWild's mobs (20–35) show up regularly but less than zombies.
  • The game log shows [Spawns] LivingWild hostile mobs registered....
  • They can be summoned with their spawn egg.

Animations

Idle, walk (adjusts to real speed), run, attack, hurt and death (the death stays visible for about 32 ticks).

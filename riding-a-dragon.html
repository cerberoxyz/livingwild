Guides

Riding a Dragon

Contents
  1. Mounting and dismounting
  2. Controls (pilot)
  3. “Do a Barrel Roll”-style flight
  4. Turbo
  5. Healing and escort
  6. Sleeping and resting
  7. Self-defense
  8. Details
Requirement: Only adult dragons can be ridden. To raise one, see Dragon Egg.

Mounting and dismounting

  • Right-click with an empty hand → mount.
  • Shift → dismount (always, both on the ground and while flying).
  • When you dismount in the air you receive Slow Falling for 10 s so you don't die.
  • Up to 2 players (like a camel): the owner pilots and the second is a passenger; the second can only get on if the owner is already aboard.
  • If the owner dismounts with someone still riding, the dragon lands slowly with the passenger.

Controls (pilot)

KeyAction
W A S DWalk
SpaceTake off and ascend
W (in the air)Move toward where the rider looks: look up = ascend, down = descend
Left-click (held)Elemental breath (range 16 blocks, 8 damage, breath animation)
Right-clickElement ball (fireball animation, released at 0.85 s)
Ctrl + WTurbo: double speed (see Turbo)
ShiftDescend
  • The head and neck turn toward where the pilot looks (up to 75° to the sides and 70° up/down). The breath and the ball come out of the mouth where the head points.
  • Without pressing Space, the dragon keeps flapping its wings in flight; without a rider, landing uses gliding.
  • When it touches the ground without pressing Space, it lands.

“Do a Barrel Roll”-style flight

  • The dragon tilts according to how fast you turn the mouse (cap of 90°) and pitches: the snout goes up or down depending on where you look.
  • The rider tilts with it (pilot and passenger) and the camera tilts toward the side of the turn.
  • If you have the Do a Barrel Roll mod, LivingWild's camera tilt turns itself off so they don't clash.
  • There are no 360° turns or full barrel rolls.

Turbo

  • Normal flight speed is 0.4 (half of what it was before v31).
  • Hold Ctrl (the sprint key) + W for turbo: double speed (0.8).
  • It lasts at most 5 s and is followed by a 5 s cooldown; the cooldown also applies if you release Ctrl early.

Healing and escort

  • Right-click with raw fish (cod, salmon or tropical fish) on an adult dragon: +10 health and Regeneration II for 10 s.
  • Right-click with cooked fish (cod or salmon): +25 health and Regeneration III for 20 s.
  • The fish is not used up if the dragon already has full health.
  • Escort: an awake adult dragon, with no rider and on the ground, watches over its owner: if someone hits the owner (in the last 5 s) or a mob targets the owner within 30 blocks, the dragon attacks it with its defense.

Sleeping and resting

  • Shift + right-click with an empty hand (on foot, owner only): the dragon lies down to sleep with a full animation (body, legs, neck, folded wings, tail, eyes closed with eyelids).
  • While asleep it does not wander or get up (not by time, not by proximity, not from hits). The same gesture wakes it up; so does mounting it.
  • It is saved with the world: if you reload, it stays asleep.
  • With no rider and no sleep order, it walks and lies down to rest on its own.

Self-defense

An unridden adult dragon attacked by a player who is not its owner, or by a mob (also with projectiles: whoever shot them counts):

  • It gets up (even if asleep), chases you and brings you down with its breath.
  • Defense damage: 100 (lethal in one hit; a totem only delays it).
  • It does not attack the owner, whoever is riding it, other dragons of the same owner, or players in creative/spectator.
  • It stops chasing if you get farther than 32 blocks or after ~30 s. If you fly out of its reach, it cannot reach you (it defends from the ground).
  • If it was sleeping by the owner's order, it goes back to sleep after the fight.
  • Dragons never hurt each other (v31): before, an ownerless dragon could be brought down by another dragon's self-defense or breath.
  • Babies do not defend themselves. With a rider, the dragon does not defend itself: the pilot does it with the breath.

Details

  • Fire does not ignite blocks by default (it doesn't burn your builds), but it does damage and set creatures on fire.
  • The second rider can now really get on (before, the game only allowed one passenger).
  • A ridden dragon is moved by the server; the client only sends the keys every tick.
  • There is no Ender chest anymore: it was tested in v21 and removed in v27.

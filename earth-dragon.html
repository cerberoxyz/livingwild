The Earth Dragon is a moss-green dragon with amber spikes and lichen-covered wings, like a living rock.

Elemental effect

Applies Poison II: 3 s with the breath, 6 s with the ball.

How to get it

  1. Look for a Dragon Portal on the outer End islands; each variant holds a different egg (25% each).
  2. Pick up the Dragon Egg (its egg gives off light level 3), take it home and place it near a heat source.
  3. Wait 5 minutes: the Baby Earth Dragon will hatch.
  4. Give it 5 raw fish (1 per minute) and it will become an adult.
  5. Ride it with an empty hand.

It can also be bred with another adult dragon (golden apple). Mixed with the right element it can produce the Magma and Crystal hybrid variants.

Baby

The Baby Earth Dragon has 40 health, walks and lies down to rest from time to time (animations rest_start → rest → rest_end); it gets up if you approach. It does not defend itself and cannot be ridden.

Adult abilities

  • Breath (held left-click): range 16 blocks.
  • Element ball (right-click): 14 damage, radius 4, cooldown 2 s, does not break blocks.
  • Turbo and healing: Ctrl + W to go at double speed; fish heals it. See Riding a Dragon.
  • Flight: Space to take off. See Riding a Dragon.

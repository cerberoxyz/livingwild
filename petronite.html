Petronite

Petronite
Information
TypeMaterial family
FoundUnderground, Y −30 to −20
IDmundovivo:petronite_bruta

Petronite is the glowing blue ore that gives the whole mod its progression. It is mined, smelted, fused with a diamond into an ingot and turned into weapons, tools, armor and blocks.

Obtaining chain

  1. Mine a Petronite Ore block with a diamond pickaxe or better. It drops Petronite Ore.
  2. Smelt it in a furnace or blast furnace (200 ticks = 10 s, 2.0 XP) to get Refined Petronite Ore. It is the same item with a different model, set through custom_model_data = "refinada".
  3. Combine refined ore + 1 diamond (shapeless) → Petronite Ingot.
  4. 9 ingots ⇄ 1 Petronite Block.

What it makes

ItemCost
Dawnblade2 ingots + 1 Blaze Rod
Stormaxe3 ingots + 2 Blaze Rods
Petronite Pickaxe3 ingots + 2 Blaze Rods
Arcane Staff8 refined ores (ring)
Petronite Armor5 / 8 / 7 / 4 ingots (helmet / chestplate / leggings / boots)
Castle Compass1 ingot + 1 compass
Void ArmorSmithing upgrade of Petronite Armor
Fairy HeartNo petronite

Ingots also repair Petronite tools and armor (anvil) and appear as rare loot from the Ogre, Burly Pig, the Hound (raw ore) and the Dragoso/Glacial bosses (blocks via code).

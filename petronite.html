Petronite
Information
TypeMaterial / item family
Obtained fromUnderground mining (Y −30 to −20)

Petronite is the blue-gray mineral that gives its name to the whole progression of the mod. It forms in veins underground, is refined in a furnace and melded with a diamond to obtain the Petronite Ingot, the basis of almost all of LivingWild's weapons, armor and tools.

Acquisition chain

  1. Find and mine a Petronite Ore with a diamond pickaxe (or better).
  2. You get Raw Petronite.
  3. Smelt the ore in a furnace or blast furnace to get Refined Petronite Ore.
  4. Combine the refined ore with a diamond (shapeless) to craft a Petronite Ingot.
  5. With 9 ingots you make a Block of Petronite (and vice versa).
